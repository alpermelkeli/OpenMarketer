1 · Questionsfrom a person or the orchestrator (a report in the project), or from another agent's research action, which waits for the findings: at most five questions, the scope (topics, competitors, a time window) and how many sources to read
2 · Scope researcher structured outputturns the questions into search queries and the kinds of source to read; code caps the queries and pages and drops domains on the project's block list
3 · Search and fetch actions, internal; the graph pauses for each
searchthrough a search provider's official API; results with their address, title and snippet
fetchpublic pages only: no login, robots.txt honoured, HTTPS, size and type limits. The page is stored; the graph gets its id, not its text.
4 · Read quarantine: the only node that sees a page
Reader node readerone page at a time, with no tools (its role may not be sent any): summary, key points, exact quotes, date, relevance to each question. Anything else is dropped; injection heuristics flag the page.
5 · Synthesize researcheranswers each question from the reader's fields only, never from a raw page; every finding names the pages it rests on and how sure it is
6 · Check codeevery finding cites a page fetched in this run; every quote is in the stored page; findings resting only on flagged pages are marked

7 · Reportstore the research report: questions, findings with their sources and when each was read, flags, who asked, cost. The outcome is its id and the findings, to the person or to the run that waits.
Findings are datathey come from untrusted pages. Whoever receives them may let them inform a plan or an angle; they never cause an action and are never the evidence of a claim about the product.
